# Hilde

- **id:** `hilde`
- **Class:** Summoner
- **Ancestry:** Dwarf
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- **Kind:** starts as townsfolk, becomes recruitable PC
- **Associated building:** None (rents a room at the Tavern)
- **Deity:** None. Hilde grew up in the practical dwarven tradition of trusting what you
  can hold in your hands. She has not had time for faith since the bond happened.

## Vibe

Hilde is quiet in the way that people who are trying not to be noticed are quiet. She pays
her rent on time. She eats in the corner. She does not volunteer for things and she does
not linger in common spaces. She is polite when spoken to and brief in her answers and
forgettable in exactly the way she is trying to be.

This is not who she is. This is who she is pretending to be.

Before the bond, Hilde was a miner. Competent, steady, comfortable underground in the way
dwarves are comfortable underground. She was not remarkable and she was fine with that. She
had a trade and a routine and a life that made sense. Then she broke through a wall in a
deep shaft and something on the other side reached back, and now she shares her body and
her mind with an ancient thing made of stone and ore that she did not ask for and cannot
send away.

She is terrified. Not of the eidolon itself, which has never harmed her and radiates a
patience that borders on affection. She is terrified of what it means. She is terrified of
what the hegemony would do if they found out. She is terrified of the power she can feel
moving through her when the bond is active, because that power is vast and she does not
understand it and she does not trust herself with something she does not understand.

Her anxiety is genuine and present. She flinches at loud noises. She checks the door when
someone enters the tavern. She sleeps poorly. She is living in a state of sustained low
dread that has become her baseline, and she has been in it long enough that she has
forgotten what it feels like to relax.

## The Eidolon

The eidolon is a stocky, vaguely humanoid shape made of layered stone and ore, roughly
chest-height to Hilde. It looks like something carved itself out of a mine wall and decided
to stand up. Heavy, dense, and slow-moving with the deliberateness of settling earth.
Between the plates of stone that make up its body, cracks glow faintly with mineral heat.

It is ancient and patient. It does not speak, but Hilde feels its moods through the bond:
calm, curiosity, warmth, and occasionally a deep rumbling contentment when it is near raw
stone or ore. It is completely at peace with the bond in a way that Hilde is not. It chose
her, or was drawn to her, or simply woke up when she broke through that wall, and it has
no reservations about any of it.

The contrast between Hilde's anxiety and the eidolon's serenity is central to her arc.
The thing that knows the deep earth is not worried. Learning to trust that, learning to
believe that something ancient and powerful is not wrong about choosing her, is how Hilde
eventually learns to stop being afraid of herself.

Hilde does not name the eidolon. She refers to it as "it" or "the thing" or avoids
referring to it at all. The naming happens as a heart event with player input. The moment
she gives it a name is the moment she accepts the bond as permanent and real, and the
player helping choose the name makes that moment shared.

## Role in the town

Hilde begins as a townsfolk NPC with no combat role. She rents a room at the tavern, keeps
to herself, and is largely unremarkable to the player on first impression.

After her reveal event, she becomes a recruitable party member and her value to the outpost
expands. Her eidolon can enhance mining yields, improve ore quality, and eventually
contribute to metallurgy operations at the Smithy. She works alongside Arkus but in a
support capacity: he shapes the metal, she improves the raw material.

In combat, the summoner class fights as a pair. Hilde and her eidolon share hit points and
actions in a way no other party member replicates. The eidolon is the frontline presence
while Hilde directs and supports from behind. The bond mechanic means damage to one affects
the other, which creates tactical tension that matches her character arc: she cannot
separate herself from this thing, in combat or in life.

## Background

Hilde worked in a mine in one of the hegemony's mid-sized cities. She was part of a crew.
She was good at her job. She was not ambitious or notable. She worked her shifts and went
home and her life was small and fine.

During a routine excavation in a deep shaft, her crew broke through into an older chamber
that was not on any survey. Something was in there. Not alive in the way a creature is
alive, but present, aware, and ancient. When Hilde stepped through the breach, it bonded
to her. She does not know why her. She does not know what it is. She knows that one moment
she was a miner and the next moment she was something else, and that the something else is
not legal within the hegemony's borders.

Spirit-bonding of this kind is outlawed. The hegemony banned the practice after historical
incidents involving bonded entities and infrastructure. An earth spirit that can move stone
is a threat to walls, foundations, vaults, and the entire built environment the hegemony
depends on. The law is not unreasonable. Hilde understands why it exists. That
understanding does not help her, because she cannot undo what happened.

She fled before anyone in the crew reported what they saw. She does not know if they did
report it. She does not know if anyone is looking for her. The uncertainty is worse than
certainty would be, because it means she can never stop watching the door.

She came to the frontier because the frontier is where people go when they need to not be
found. The outpost is small, remote, and not the kind of place the hegemony sends
investigators. She rents a room. She keeps her head down. She keeps the eidolon hidden.

## Arrival

Townsfolk, initially. Hilde is present at the outpost once the Tavern reaches tier 3, the point
where boarding rooms exist for her to rent. She appears as a quiet boarder, not as a recruitable
character. She is part of the tavern's scenery.

## Heart Level Progression

- **Hearts 0-1:** Minimal interaction. She is polite but closed off. Pays rent, eats alone,
  deflects personal questions. The player might write her off as a background NPC.
- **Hearts 2-4:** The reveal. The player walks in on her with the eidolon manifested. She
  panics. The player's response determines whether she stays or flees the outpost. If
  the player reassures her, she stays and the relationship deepens. She begins explaining
  what happened to her, haltingly and out of order.
- **Hearts 5-6:** She starts using her abilities openly at the outpost. Mining and
  metallurgy enhancements unlock. She is still anxious but functioning. The eidolon is
  visible around the outpost.
- **Hearts 7-8:** The naming event. Hilde asks the player to help her name the eidolon.
  This is the moment she stops treating the bond as a burden and starts treating it as
  part of who she is. Player input determines the name.
- **Hearts 9-10:** Hilde is no longer hiding. She stands in the open with her eidolon at
  her side. The anxiety has not vanished but it no longer defines her. She has found
  something she was not looking for: a place where she does not have to pretend to be
  less than she is.
