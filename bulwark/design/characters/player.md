# The Player Character

- **id:** `player`
- **Class:** Fighter
- **Ancestry:** Human
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Kind:** starting-party PC (player-named at game start)
- **Associated building:** none (generalist)

## Vibe

Practical and steady. He knows the land because he grew up working it, and he is more comfortable
with a hoe than a salute. There is a soldier's discipline in him now, layered over the farmhand
he used to be, but nothing about him is flashy or loud. He simply gets things done.

## Role in the town

The player's avatar. He drives the farming, the outpost expansion, and the building upgrades. He
is not tied to any one building but instead serves as the generalist who keeps everything moving.
His job, broadly, is to turn this neglected outpost into something worth defending.

## Background

A farmhand who joined the army and was sent to reinforce a crumbling frontier outpost on the
kingdom's border. He arrives with two companions, the elf rogue and the halfling wizard, and
finds Tharr holding the place together alone.

## Arrival

Starting party. Present from day one.
