# Credits

## Data

- **OpenJLPT** (https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT), CC BY-SA 4.0. JLPT level per kanji and word, word readings and meanings.
  - Vocabulary readings and glosses derive from **JMdict/EDICT**, Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG), used under the EDRDG licence (CC BY-SA). https://www.edrdg.org/
  - Level assignments derive from **Jonathan Waller's JLPT resources** (https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/), CC BY. No official JLPT lists exist; these are community lists.
- **KANJIDIC2**, EDRDG, used under the EDRDG licence (CC BY-SA). Kanji meanings, readings, Kangxi radicals, stroke counts, frequency. https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/KANJIDIC_Project.html
- **KRADFILE**, EDRDG, used under the EDRDG licence (CC BY-SA). Visible components per kanji.
- **KanjiVG**, copyright Ulrich Apel, CC BY-SA 3.0. Stroke order paths. https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/

The data files in `data/` are derived from the above and are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
