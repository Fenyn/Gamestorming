# Aveline, the Lady of Dawn

Aveline is the goddess who kindled the first light. In the age before the Crown, when men
huddled in the long shadow and the dead walked freely, she set her hand against the horizon
and the sun came. With it came warmth, and the slow green mending of all wounded things.

## Core identity

- **Title:** The Lady of Dawn
- **Domains:** Fire, Healing, Sun, Truth
- **Divine font:** Heal
- **Sanctification:** Holy
- **Divine skill:** Medicine
- **Favored weapon:** Scimitar

## Favored weapon: the scimitar

The curved blade represents the arc of the sun across the sky. Aveline's faithful carry
scimitars as both weapon and symbol, and the finest are polished to catch the light. There is
a tradition among her followers of drawing the blade at dawn and holding it until it catches the
first ray of sunlight, a brief daily devotion that doubles as a practical reminder to rise early.

## Edicts

- Destroy the undead
- Bring light to dark places
- Offer mercy to those who surrender

## Anathema

- Create undead
- Abandon the wounded
- Extinguish a source of light in the darkness

## Theology

Aveline is a goddess of compassion expressed through action. Her followers do not sit in
contemplation. They carry lanterns into dark places and offer the open hand before the closed
fist. She is patron of healers and of soldiers who fight without cruelty, of those who believe
that mending what is broken is a higher calling than breaking what stands.

Her worship ran through the Crown like a golden thread in the years of Arthur's reign, and her
chapels rose tall in every city and hamlet along the borderlands. Now many of those chapels stand
half-ruined, their congregations scattered by war and abandonment, tended only by the faithful
few who will not leave.

Sister Cael keeps a shrine to Aveline in the outpost chapel. Maren carries her light into
battle. These names belong to the old fiction from the original Tactics project and may be
reused for Bulwark NPCs.

## Granted spells

- **Rank 1:** Breathe Fire
- **Rank 3:** Fireball

## Notes

Aveline was originally ported from the Unity Tactics project (ScriptableObjects/Deities/
Aveline.asset). She is fully implemented in the engine via PresetDeities.cs. Tharr has since
switched to Valdûr, so Aveline is currently unassigned among the starting party but remains
available for future characters, particularly anyone associated with the Chapel building.
