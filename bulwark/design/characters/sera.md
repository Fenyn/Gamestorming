# Sera

- **id:** `sera`
- **Class:** Magus
- **Ancestry:** Human
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- **Kind:** recruitable PC
- **Associated building:** Arcane Study
- **Deity:** None. Sera considers faith in the divine to be an unstudied assumption. She does
  not deny the existence of gods. She denies that anyone has done the work to prove they
  function the way clerics claim they do. This has not made her popular with Tharr.

## Vibe

Sera is the smartest person in the room and she does not know how to pretend otherwise. This
is not arrogance. She does not think less of other people. She operates at a speed and
depth that most people cannot follow, and it has never occurred to her to slow down. She asks
questions the way other people breathe: constantly, involuntarily, and with no regard for
whether the person she is asking wants to think that hard right now.

She is a compulsive academic. Her quarters are covered in diagrams, notes, half-finished
proofs, and experimental apparatus in various states of assembly. She forgets to eat when she
is working. She forgets to sleep. She does not forget the exact energy signature of a spell she
observed six weeks ago, because that is the kind of information her mind considers important.

She is warm underneath the intensity. She likes people. She likes explaining things to people
who want to learn. She lights up when someone asks her a good question, and she is generous
with her knowledge in a way that the hegemony's academic establishment never was with her. The
problem is that her warmth is delivered at the same relentless pace as everything else, and
most people find it exhausting before they find it endearing.

## Role in the town

Sera runs the Arcane Study. She researches spells, produces scrolls, and serves as the
outpost's authority on all things arcane. Where Fenwick uses magic intuitively and joyfully,
Sera dissects it. As the Arcane Study upgrades, the depth of her research expands: new spells
available to the party, Recall Knowledge bonuses, and eventually the ability to identify and
deconstruct magical phenomena encountered in the field.

In combat, she is precise and devastating. The Magus class channels spells through weapon
strikes, and Sera treats every Spellstrike as a live experiment. She is not reckless. She is
methodical. She knows exactly what the energy will do when it meets the blade and exactly what
the blade will do when it meets the target, because she has studied both interactions more
thoroughly than anyone else alive.

## Background

Sera came up through the hegemony's arcane academies. She was brilliant from the start, the
kind of student who asks questions that make instructors pause and then quietly revise their
lecture notes. Her special interest is the underlying fabric of arcane magic: not individual
spells or schools, but the structure that holds all of it together. What is the medium through
which arcane energy propagates? Why do certain geometries produce consistent effects? What are
the actual rules, not the traditions, not the conventions, but the rules?

This made her exceptional. It also made her a problem. The hegemony's arcane establishment is
old, credentialed, and deeply invested in its own framework. Sera's research kept arriving at
conclusions that contradicted established theory, and she kept publishing them because they
were correct and she did not understand why correctness would be unwelcome. She dismantled a
senior mage's foundational treatise with enough evidence that it could not be dismissed, and
she genuinely did not understand why that made the senior mage her enemy. She was not trying
to embarrass anyone. She was trying to be accurate. The fact that accuracy was embarrassing
was, to her, everyone else's problem.

The establishment did not formally expel her. They did something quieter: withdrew access to
research archives, declined her funding proposals, stopped inviting her to symposia, made it
clear through a hundred small exclusions that she was no longer welcome. Sera left because
there was nothing left to stay for. The frontier has no academies, no politics, no one telling
her which questions are safe to ask. It also has no one who can keep up with her, but she has
made her peace with that.

## Arrival

Recruitable. Sera arrives at the outpost deliberately, having heard of it through frontier
trade networks. A growing settlement on the border between two powers, in territory where the
old rules are thin and the land has not been studied — that is exactly the kind of place where
someone like Sera can do her best work. She shows up with a cart full of books, instruments,
and half-finished experiments, asks where she can set up a workspace, and starts working before
anyone has formally agreed to let her stay. The trigger is the Trading Post reaching tier 2, the
expanded store that means enough goods and enough word are moving through the outpost to have
reached the trade networks she was listening to in the first place.

- Build: Magus + TBD FA line. Likely Str or Dex 18 with Int 18 secondary. Spellstrike is the
  core mechanic: channel a spell through a melee or ranged Strike. One-handed weapon to keep
  a hand free for spellcasting.
- Magus is not on the planned class list. Needs adding to the README. Spellstrike, Arcane
  Cascade, conflux spells, and hybrid study abilities will need implementation.
- Her relationship with Fenwick is the most natural academic pairing. He uses magic intuitively
  and she finds that both fascinating and maddening. He can do things he cannot explain, and
  she cannot rest until she has explained them. He thinks she is overthinking it. She thinks
  he is underthinking it. They are both right.
- Her relationship with Tharr is friction. She questions divine magic with the same rigor she
  questions arcane magic, and Tharr's faith is not something he subjects to peer review. This
  tension is productive, not hostile, but it is real.
- Her relationship with Josen could be unexpectedly rich. He has pursued mastery of the body
  the way she pursues mastery of magic. They are both people who have gone further than their
  traditions could take them and are now working alone. The difference is that Josen has found
  peace in the unknown and Sera cannot tolerate it.
- Her special interest in the fabric of magic could tie into larger worldbuilding: what is the
  relationship between arcane magic, divine magic, and primal magic? Are they the same
  energy expressed differently, or fundamentally separate phenomena? Sera has opinions. They
  are not popular.
