# Tharr

- **id:** `tharr`
- **Class:** Cleric (Warpriest)
- **Ancestry:** Dwarf
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Kind:** starting-party PC
- **Associated building:** Command Post (construction / planning table)
- **Deity:** Valdûr, the Stonefather (see `../deities/valdur.md`)

## Vibe
Gruff but dependable. Takes pride in solid craftsmanship — if a wall's going up, it's going up
right. Quietly relieved to finally have help, though he won't say it outright. Stoic on the
surface, stubborn underneath. His faith is in his hands and his hammer.

## Role in the town
Construction-master. Leads building repair, restoration, and new construction. Operates the
planning table at the Command Post. He's the one who knows what the outpost needs structurally
and how to get it done. When he's not swinging a hammer at a wall, he's swinging one at whatever
threatens the walls he built.

## Background
A dwarven stonemason and cleric of Valdûr, stationed alone at this crumbling outpost. Has been
struggling to hold it together on his own — patching walls, keeping the roof from caving in,
praying the stones hold. His warhammer has seen more masonry than combat, but it works fine for
both. When the player arrives with reinforcements, the outpost finally has a chance.

## Arrival
Starting party — already at the outpost when the player arrives. The lone holdout.
