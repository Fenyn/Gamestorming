- **id:** `player`

## Vibe
Practical and steady. Knows the land — grew up working it. More comfortable with a hoe than a
salute, but carries a soldier's discipline. Not flashy, not loud. Gets things done.

## Role in the town
The player's avatar. Drives farming (Stardew-style crop growing), outpost expansion, and building
upgrades. Not tied to any one building — the generalist who keeps everything moving. Tasked with
turning this neglected outpost into something worth defending.

## Background
A farmhand who joined the army. Sent to reinforce a crumbling frontier outpost on the kingdom's
border. Arrives with two companions (the elf rogue and the halfling wizard) to find Tharr holding
the place together alone.

## Arrival
Starting party — present from day one.
