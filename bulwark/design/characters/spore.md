# Spore

- **id:** `spore`
- **Class:** Witch
- **Ancestry:** Leshy (Fungus)
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- **Kind:** recruitable PC
- **Associated building:** Apothecary
- **Patron:** Grandmother Mulch (see `../patrons/grandmother_mulch.md`)

## Vibe

Spore is strange in the way that a talking mushroom who brews potions in a walking cauldron
is strange, which is to say completely and without apology. She does not understand why people
build walls between themselves and the rain. She does not understand money. She does not
understand why anyone would cook meat when there are perfectly good lichen right there on that
rock. She is not stupid. She is operating from a set of assumptions so different from everyone
else's that the gaps look like ignorance from the outside.

She is curious about everything and afraid of almost nothing. She talks to her cauldron the way
other people talk to a dog, and the cauldron talks back in its own way, rattling its lid when
it is excited and going cold when it disapproves. She collects things: interesting bark, stones
with unusual colors, bones that still smell like the animal they came from. Her workspace is
a disaster by any civilized standard and a perfectly organized system by hers.

She is the first leshy anyone in the party has ever met. Most of them did not know leshies
existed.

## Role in the town

Spore runs the Apothecary. She brews healing potions, antidotes, elixirs, and other
consumables from foraged ingredients. As the Apothecary upgrades and her ingredient library
grows, the range and potency of what she can produce expands. She is the outpost's only source
of alchemical consumables.

She fills the outsider role in the settlement. She is not hostile or antisocial. She simply
operates on a different wavelength from everyone else. She does not attend meetings. She does
not care about the economy. She cares about the forest, her brews, and the small living things
that most people step on without noticing. Over time, the other residents learn to appreciate
this perspective, or at least to stop being startled when a three-foot mushroom person walks
out of the treeline carrying a bucket of slugs.

## Background

Leshies are immortal nature spirits inhabiting small plant bodies, brought into being through
primal ritual or, in rare cases, through the will of something older and stranger. Spore is
the latter. Grandmother Mulch chose the vessel and called the spirit into it. Spore does not
remember any life before this one. She knows the forest because the forest is all she has ever
known. She knows Grandmother Mulch the way a root knows the soil: not as a separate thing but
as the medium she exists within.

She has lived in the deep forest for as long as she can remember, which could be decades or
centuries. Time does not organize itself the same way for her. She measures it in bloom cycles
and fruiting seasons, not in years. She has never seen a town. She has met travelers before but
only in the way that a fox meets travelers, by watching from a distance and occasionally
following them for a while.

She was found by the outpost's patrol in the deep forest, crouched over her cauldron, brewing
something that smelled terrible and worked remarkably well. She is not lost. She is not in
danger. She is simply there, doing what she does, and if these strange tall creatures want to
give her a roof and a steady supply of interesting ingredients, she sees no reason to object.

## The cauldron

Spore's familiar is an animated cauldron. It is small enough to carry, though it prefers to
walk on its own stubby legs. It hops, waddles, and occasionally rolls when it builds up too
much momentum. Its lid clatters when it is excited and clamps shut when it senses danger. It
does not speak, but Spore understands it perfectly and translates its moods for anyone who
asks.

The cauldron is the vessel through which Grandmother Mulch channels her patron magic. It is
always warm. Things left inside it tend to dissolve into useful components overnight. Spore
feeds it scraps and forest litter the way other people feed a pet, and it seems to appreciate
the attention.

## Notes (deferred / later)

- Build: Witch + TBD FA line. Witch is not yet in the supported engine class list. Patron
  grants a familiar, a tradition (Primal for Grandmother Mulch), and hex cantrips. The
  animated cauldron is a flavor-specific familiar, not a mechanical exception.
- Fungus Leshy heritage gives darkvision and familiarity with decay/decomposition, which fits
  the patron thematically.
- Leshy ancestry is not yet in the supported list. Needs adding to the README.
- Her lack of worldly knowledge is a character trait, not a limitation to overcome. She should
  not have an arc where she "learns to be normal." She should have an arc where the outpost
  learns to value what she brings.
- Alchemy in PF2e is typically an Alchemist class feature, but the Apothecary building can
  abstract this as a crafting system rather than a class mechanic. Spore brews things
  because that is what she does, not because of a class feature.
