# Raven (Vivienne Blackwood)

- **id:** `raven`
- **Class:** Swashbuckler (Braggart)
- **Ancestry:** Human
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- **Kind:** recruitable PC (missable)
- **Associated building:** None
- **Deity:** None. Raven does not kneel. She has a vague respect for whatever god
  oversees luck and daring, but she has never bothered to learn their name.

## Vibe

Raven is the loudest person in any room and she is loud on purpose. She announces herself
when she enters. She tells you what she is about to do before she does it. She tells you
how impressive it was afterward. This is not insecurity. She is genuinely that good, and
she knows it, and she sees no reason to pretend otherwise. Humility is a virtue for people
who have something to be humble about.

She fights the way she talks: big, flashy, and overwhelming. The Braggart style is not an
affectation. Demoralizing an opponent before the blade lands is a tactical choice, and she
has refined it into an art form. She tells an enemy exactly how the next ten seconds are
going to go, and then she makes it happen, and the look on their face when they realize
she was not exaggerating is the part she lives for.

Underneath the performance is someone who is genuinely competent and knows the weight of
that competence. She does not take contracts she cannot finish. She does not pick fights
she cannot win. The bravado reads as reckless but her judgment is precise. She has survived
years as a solo bounty hunter in frontier territory, and that does not happen by accident.

She is also, beneath everything, having the time of her life. She left behind wealth and
comfort and obligation, and what she got in return was freedom and danger and the
satisfaction of being good at something that matters in the moment it happens. She would
rather die in a ditch with a sword in her hand than grow old in a manor pretending to care
about table settings. She means this sincerely and she has made peace with what it costs.

## Role in the town

Raven has no building assignment. She is a pure combat recruit whose value is in the field.
In a party she is a melee striker who weakens enemies for the rest of the team. Frightened
enemies take penalties to everything, and Raven's Demoralize-into-finisher loop means she
is constantly applying pressure that benefits all adjacent allies.

She does not contribute to outpost infrastructure. She is not interested in building things.
She is interested in the things that building an outpost attracts: bounties, monsters,
trouble worth getting into. The outpost is her base camp, not her project.

## Background

Raven's real name is Vivienne Blackwood. The Blackwood family is old money in the hegemony,
the kind of family whose name appears on buildings and whose opinions shape policy in
whatever city they currently occupy. Vivienne was raised with tutors, formal combat
instruction, etiquette training, and the understanding that her future involved a political
marriage, a household to manage, and a role in the family's ongoing project of being
important.

She was good at the combat training. She was terrible at everything else, not because she
lacked the ability but because she could not make herself care. The fencing tutors were the
only part of her education that felt real. Everything else was performance for an audience
she did not respect. She endured it until she was old enough to leave, and then she left.

She does not talk about this. The Blackwood name would attract exactly the kind of
attention she abandoned that life to escape. Bounty hunters and treasure seekers named
Vivienne Blackwood get retrieved by family retainers. Bounty hunters named Raven get left
alone. The name is a practical choice as much as a personal one.

Her combat skill is real but its foundation is privilege. She had the best instructors the
hegemony could provide, and those instructors gave her a technical base that most
self-taught fighters never develop. She built on that foundation with years of practical
experience, but the foundation itself was bought with family money. This is the one thing
she does not brag about. If pressed at high friendship, she admits it with visible
discomfort. She wants to believe she built herself from nothing, and the truth is more
complicated than that.

The irony of a Blackwood going by Raven has not occurred to her. If someone pointed it out,
she would be furious.

## Arrival

Recruitable, missable. Raven begins appearing at the outpost once the Trading Post and the
Tavern are both built and a day threshold has passed. She uses the Trading Post to
sell loot and buy supplies. She uses the Tavern for a hot meal. She is a customer.

Her visits are periodic and initially brief. She is friendly in the way that people who are
good at talking to strangers are friendly: warm on the surface, nothing underneath it yet.
She has stories. She will tell them. She will not ask about yours.

The player can build friendship through gifts and conversation during her visits. As
friendship grows, her visits get longer and her interest in the outpost shifts from
transactional to personal. She starts asking about the people. She lingers after her
business is done. She picks fights with local monsters "because she was in the area anyway."

At the hearts 5-6 recruitment threshold, she offers to stay. She frames it as a business decision.
The outpost is centrally located for her work, the facilities are decent, and having a
steady base of operations beats sleeping in the woods. Everyone at the outpost knows this
is not the real reason. She knows they know. Nobody says it out loud.

If the player never invests in the relationship, she remains a recurring visitor and
occasional combat encounter but never joins. She is entirely missable as a party member.

## Heart Level Progression

- **Hearts 0-2:** Surface-level charm. Stories about bounties and close calls. She is
  entertaining but reveals nothing personal.
- **Hearts 3-4:** She starts showing opinions. Comments on the outpost, its people, the
  player's choices. Still guarded but engaged.
- **Hearts 5-6:** Recruitment threshold. She joins the outpost. Begins hinting at a life
  before bounty hunting without specifics.
- **Hearts 7-8:** The real name. She tells the player her name is Vivienne Blackwood, and
  the weight of saying it out loud is the most genuine moment she has had in years. She
  explains what she left and why. No regret, but real vulnerability.
- **Hearts 9-10:** She has stopped performing. The bravado is still there because it is
  genuinely who she is, but the walls behind it are down. She talks about what she actually
  wants from her life, not just what she is running from. The outpost is home and she has
  stopped pretending it is not.
