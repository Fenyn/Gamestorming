# Elara

- **id:** `elara`
- **Class:** Rogue (Thief racket)
- **Ancestry:** Elf
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- **Kind:** starting-party PC
- **Associated building:** Trading Post
- **Deity:** None (secular)

## Vibe

Elara is a silver tongue. She is the kind of person who makes a deal feel like a favor, who can
talk her way past a locked gate faster than she could pick it. There is genuine warmth in the
charm, but there is also calculation. She reads a room the way a jeweler reads a gem, quickly
and with an eye for value. She is well-traveled and worldly, the sort who has been to enough
places to know what things are worth and what people will pay for them.

She is not dishonest, exactly. She simply understands that the truth has many angles, and that
some of those angles are more profitable than others.

## Role in the town

Elara runs the Trading Post. She handles buying, selling, and the flow of goods in and out of
the outpost. As the settlement grows and the Smithy produces higher-quality equipment, her stock
expands to match. She is the economic engine of the outpost, the one who turns surplus into
opportunity.

Beyond trade, she is the party's social fulcrum. Where the player builds and Tharr repairs,
Elara connects. She knows what travelers want, what merchants need, and what information is
worth keeping close.

## Background

Elara joined the player's company on the road to the outpost, though her reasons for heading
to a neglected frontier post are her own. She claims the kingdom is offering favorable trade
concessions to anyone willing to establish commerce in the borderlands, and that is probably
true, but probably not the whole story either. She is an elf, which means she has been doing
this longer than the others have been alive, and she carries that experience lightly.

## Arrival

Starting party. Travels with the player to the outpost.
