# The Player Character

- **id:** `player`
- **Class:** Fighter
- **Ancestry:** Human
- **Age:** ~25
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Kind:** starting-party PC (player-named at game start)
- **Associated building:** none (generalist)

## Vibe

Practical and steady. He grew up working the land and is more comfortable with a hoe than a
salute. The academy gave him polish: he stands right, keeps his kit in order, knows the drill
forms cold. None of it has been tested. What reads as a soldier's calm is a farm kid's work
ethic wearing a uniform, and the difference will not show until something goes wrong.

His belief in the crown is sincere. He grew up where the songs still match the scenery, and no
one he knew had reason to doubt them.

## Role in the town

The player's avatar. He drives the farming, the outpost expansion, and the building upgrades. He
is not tied to any one building but instead serves as the generalist who keeps everything moving.
His job, broadly, is to turn this neglected outpost into something worth defending.

## Background

He was raised on a farm a day's ride from the capital, close enough that Aurenthal still looks
like its own songs. His parents work that land still, and they were proud when he enlisted. The
recruiters described Lanric's kingdom: a garrisoned frontier, chapels in every hamlet, dwarven
roads running to the edge of the map. Every word of it was true a generation ago, and nobody in
his life could tell him the tense was wrong.

At the academy he trained in the martial hall while Fenwick studied in the arcane hall. They
met over food in the dormitory kitchen and became friends despite their temperaments. When
postings came, cadets with house patronage drew capital garrisons. He had no house, so he drew
the frontier: a border outpost the ministries had ignored for eight years, finally answered
with the smallest thing that would close the file. Two academy graduates, no wagon train, no
relief schedule. Tharr asked for a garrison and got them. On paper, their commanding officer
is the outpost's warden of record: a dwarven mason who never wanted command.

This is his first post. He arrives expecting the frontier of the songs and finds what the
contraction left. The game's arc is his answer to that: the pitch was hollow, but the work is
real, and he builds the thing he was promised already existed.
