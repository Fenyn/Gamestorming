# Vasska

- **id:** `vasska`
- **Class:** Psychic
- **Ancestry:** Nagaji
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- **Kind:** recruitable PC (missable, gated behind Oskar 6/10 hearts)
- **Associated building:** None
- **Deity:** None. Vasska's relationship with the world is entirely internal. She does not
  worship. She observes.

## Vibe

Every interaction with Vasska feels like standing on the edge of a trance. Her unblinking
serpentine eyes fix on you with absolute focus, and there is a sensation, not unpleasant but
not comfortable, of your thoughts being gently held in place. You are aware, on some level
beneath language, that you could slip under if you stopped resisting. That if you let go, you
would float in the stillness of her attention until she released you.

This is Vasska saying hello. She has no idea it is unsettling.

To her, this is how nagaji communicate. The intensity, the psychic weight, the
trance-edge that makes mammals want to either flee or submit, is as natural and unremarkable
as a handshake. She does not modulate it because it has never occurred to her that it needs
modulating. When people flinch or lose their train of thought mid-conversation, she waits
patiently for them to collect themselves and then continues, unaware that she is the reason
they lost it in the first place.

All of Vasska's communication is telepathic. She does not speak aloud. Her words arrive
directly in the listener's mind, fully formed, carrying not just meaning but texture: the
warmth of her interest, the weight of her focus, the faint pull of something deeper beneath
the surface. Her dialogue should render differently from all other characters to indicate
that the player is hearing her in their mind rather than through their ears.

Behind the intensity is someone genuinely curious and without malice. She is fascinated by
the minds around her the way a naturalist is fascinated by an unfamiliar ecosystem. Every
person at the outpost thinks differently, feels differently, processes the world through a
structure she has never encountered before. She finds this wonderful. She studies it with the
same unwavering focus she brings to everything, which is to say: too much focus, delivered
without warning, in a way that makes the subject feel like a pinned butterfly.

## Role in the town

Vasska has no building assignment. She is a party member whose value is in combat and in the
Oskar ritual questline. In the field, she is an occult caster who manipulates perception,
emotion, and cognition. She does not hit hard. She makes the enemy forget why they were
fighting, or believe they are somewhere else, or just stop.

Her psychic abilities also make her uniquely suited to support roles that no other character
can fill: reading intentions during negotiations, sensing threats before they materialize,
and the delicate mental surgery required to untangle a divine curse from a dying dwarf's mind.

## Background

Vasska is from a nagaji community deep in the swamp biome. Her people are reclusive, rarely
seen by outsiders, and largely unknown to the hegemony and the elven nations alike. They have
their own culture, their own traditions, and their own understanding of the world, none of
which maps to anything the party has encountered before. A nagaji at the frontier is not just
unusual. For most of the party, it is the first proof that nagaji exist at all.

Nagaji communicate telepathically as a matter of course. Among her own people, Vasska's
psychic presence is normal. Outside her community, it registers as something between hypnosis
and a threat display. She does not understand this reaction because she has never had to. The
swamp kept her people isolated enough that the question of how other ancestries experience
nagaji communication never came up at all.

Vasska left the swamp for her own reasons. She is a psychic of unusual strength, and her
abilities drew her outward, toward minds she had never touched, structures of thought she had
never encountered. She is not on a mission. She is not fleeing. She is exploring the way
a deeply focused specialist explores: methodically, thoroughly, and with a level of
personal investment that other people find intense because it is intense.

## Arrival

Recruitable, missable. Vasska's encounter in the swamp biome is gated behind reaching 6/10
hearts with Oskar. Until that threshold is met, she does not appear.

When the player explores the swamp biome after triggering the gate, they encounter Vasska.
She is not hostile, not hiding, not in distress. She is present, observing, the way
she observes everything. Recruiting her requires a subquest to befriend her. She is not
immediately willing to leave the swamp. The player must give her a reason, and "we need your
help" is not sufficient. She needs to find the outpost interesting enough to warrant her
attention.

If the player never reaches 6/10 hearts with Oskar, or never explores the swamp biome, or
chooses not to pursue the subquest, Vasska is never recruited. She is entirely missable.

## Oskar Connection

If Oskar is present at the outpost when Vasska arrives, she notices him immediately. The
divine signal bleeding out of his mind is, to her, deafening. She cannot ignore it any more
than a person could ignore someone screaming in a quiet room. She approaches the player about
it unprompted, identifies what is happening to Oskar's mind, and suggests that a ritual might
stabilize him, but that she cannot do it alone. She would need specialists.

This begins the ritual quest. See `oskar.md` for full quest design.
