# Tutorial card art

`tutorial_personality_01.png` (the Straw Knight): the Scarecrow prop from LimeZu's Modern Farm pack
(`Sprites/Farming/Modern_Farm_v1.2/48x48/Single_Files_48x48/Props_and_Buildings_48x48/Scarecrow_48x48.png`
in the local art library), trimmed to its pixels, scaled four times with nearest-neighbour sampling
and centred on a transparent 604 x 868 portrait canvas.
