Exact prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create one Grizzly Bear construction reference sprite for Delve. Supplied lineup is rendering and native pixel-scale reference only: broad uniform color regions, one shadow, deep overlap accents, sparse short upper contour highlights. New animal must be a heavy stocky grizzly on four grounded broad plantigrade paws, horizontal ribcage, unmistakable shoulder hump above rump, short thick neck, rounded ears, broad head and short tan muzzle. Face RIGHT in coherent side/three-quarter view, visible near and far legs. No roaring or standing upright. Brown coat with 7-9 flat colors; no texture, dithering, gradients, continuous black outline, decorative marks or gear. One full body pose centered with padding on genuinely transparent background. Bear should occupy roughly 62 by 40 native pixels, constructed from deliberate stepped clusters, larger and heavier than the reference wolf. Anatomy and readable silhouette are the purpose; final will be reconstructed on native grid in Aseprite.

Review revisions:

- The reference established a recognizable shoulder hump, round ears, low head, and four heavy paws; its noisy color variation and jagged fur shadows did not match the existing suite.
- Initial 62-pixel-wide reconstruction was too close to the regular wolf's dimensions. Resampled at 76 by 46 on a 96 by 64 canvas, keeping the project's .02 world units per pixel and four-pixel foot margin.
- Repainted the upper ribcage into broad coat and shadow planes, removing the repeated jagged fur marks. Replaced the long upper highlight with two short accents.
- Replaced the initial body shove with articulated near-foreleg poses: folded anticipation, forward claw swipe, low follow-through, exact rest recovery. Completed rear chest anatomy behind the lifted limb and reviewed the exposed boundary.
- Nine native layers include two deliberately empty slots (occluded tail and future equipment); the seven painted layers separate near/far limbs, chest, head and facial accents. No hidden complete tail is claimed.
