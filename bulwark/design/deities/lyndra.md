# Lyndra

Halfling goddess of kinship, the hearth, and the shared meal. Her worship is woven so thoroughly
into halfling daily life that many halflings do not even think of it as worship. Setting an extra
place at the table, leaving the fire burning for a late traveler, splitting the last loaf rather
than keeping it whole: these are all acts of Lyndra's faith.

## Core identity

- **Title:** None commonly used. Some say "the Hearthkeeper" but most halflings just say Lyndra.
- **Domains:** Family, Fire, Indulgence, Protection
- **Divine font:** Heal
- **Sanctification:** Optional good (Lyndra's faith is about generosity and warmth rather than
  moral crusade, though her followers tend naturally toward kindness)
- **Divine skill:** Diplomacy
- **Favored weapon:** Sickle

## Favored weapon: the sickle

The sickle is a harvest tool. Halflings who follow Lyndra see it the same way the Valdûrites
see the warhammer: the tool came first, and the weapon came after. A sickle cuts grain, trims
herbs, clears brush for a new garden. That it can also defend the home and the people in it is
not a contradiction but a natural extension of the same care.

Sickle stats (PF2e Remaster): 1d4 S, 1 hand, Agile/Finesse/Trip traits. Simple weapon.

## Edicts

- Feed those who are hungry, especially strangers
- Keep a warm hearth and an open door
- Share what you have before hoarding what you might need
- Remember the names and stories of those who sit at your table

## Anathema

- Turn away someone in need of shelter or food
- Eat well while others nearby go without
- Break the trust of a guest or a host
- Forget the people who raised you

## Theology

Lyndra keeps no grand temples and guards no ancient mysteries. She is the warmth of a kitchen on a
cold evening. She is the reason halflings set one more chair than they need and cook a little
more than they can eat. Her followers believe she is present wherever people gather to share food
and company, and absent wherever they eat alone by choice rather than necessity.

The halflings do not believe Lyndra created them. They believe she chose them, the way you might
choose to sit beside someone at a crowded inn. She saw them and liked them and decided to stay,
and that has made all the difference. There is no creation myth, no cosmic origin story. Just a
goddess who showed up, sat down, and never left.

Unlike other religions, her faith has no formal clergy. Any halfling who cooks for others
is performing a rite. Any home that welcomes a stranger is a temple. The most devout followers
of Lyndra are the ones who cook the most and share the most freely. Among halfling
communities, the greatest honor is to be known as someone who keeps a good table.

"A full belly and a warm fire. What else does anyone need?"

## Notes

Lyndra's faith is perfectly suited to Fenwick's personality. He would see no gap between his
devotion to cooking and his devotion to Lyndra. For him, the kitchen IS the temple, and every
meal is an offering.
