# Pre-duel lead-ins, draft for review

Fifth pass, 2026-09-25, rewritten in the fighting-game style. The model is BlazBlue's intros,
with real examples in the review notes. Nothing here is in the data. Lines marked [NEW] add a
fact that is not in `cast.md`.

## Style

1. **The narration line gives context.** It says who these two are to each other and why they
   are fighting, in plain words. It is not mood or scenery.
2. **Two spoken lines, sometimes three.** Short lines, mostly under eight words.
3. **People raise their voices.** Exclamations, stammers and trailing off are all fine.
4. **Names and nicknames carry the relationship**: "Father!", "LORD Mourne!", "boy", "Sir
   Kneels-a-Lot".
5. **Feelings are said out loud.** Nobody hides behind dry wit, and there are no quotable last
   words.
6. **Each character has one voice**, recognisable without the name tag.
7. **A lore hint is a bare name or detail that nobody explains.**

| Who | Voice |
|---|---|
| Edric | Steady and tired. First names, "son". Refuses to rise to insults |
| Alder | Loud and commanding. Full names when angry. Scolds |
| Emrys | Eager. "Father!". Wants to be taken seriously |
| Caedan | Almost silent. "Guard!", "...", "Rooke" |
| Mourne | Arrogant and mocking. "LORD Mourne!", "boy", "Sir Kneels-a-Lot" |
| Marrow | "Some of me...", "my lord!" |
| Sable | Brash captain. "Gideon!", "darling", deals and dares |
| Kell | Old officer. "My lord." Short, dutiful |
| Ashmark | A word or two. Hunger. Trails off, or shouts |
| Siphon | No contractions. Polite when fed, frantic when starving |
| Quarr | Few words. "Go home." "Lad." Never explains |

Slots per pair: **First**, **Again**, **After a loss** (they beat you last time), then **Boss**
and **Boss again** (after losing to them), or **Act 3** and **Level** (the main has levelled up).

## Sir Edric Rooke

### vs Dame Alder Rooke

**First**
> Edric meets his wife Alder for the first time since he left for the Heart without telling her.

**Alder:** Edric Rooke! You went after the Heart without one word to me!
**Edric:** I left a note!
**Alder:** For Wren! Shield up, you coward!

**Again**
> Alder again. She hasn't forgiven him, and she's brought the whole coven to watch.

**Alder:** Again, Edric! And keep your left side up this time!
**Edric:** Yes, dear.

**After a loss**
> Alder beat her husband last time, and the coven hasn't stopped talking about it.

**Edric:** Go easy on me this time, Alder.
**Alder:** Not a chance, old man!

**Act 3**
