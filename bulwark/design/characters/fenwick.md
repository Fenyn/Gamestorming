# Fenwick

- **id:** `fenwick`
- **Class:** Wizard (School of Battle Magic)
- **Ancestry:** Halfling
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Kind:** starting-party PC
- **Associated building:** Tavern
- **Deity:** Lyndra (see `../deities/lyndra.md`)

## Vibe

Fenwick is friendly in the way that makes other people friendly. He talks to everyone, remembers
what they like to eat, and has an opinion about the proper way to prepare almost anything. He is
chipper without being grating, largely because his enthusiasm is genuine rather than performed.
He calls himself the world's first gastronomancer, and while no one else uses that word, no one
has been able to argue with the results.

He is clever in the way that people who love learning tend to be. He picks things up quickly,
asks good questions, and remembers details. His parents sent him to wizard college, and he
graduated, but his heart was never in the arcane theory. It was in the kitchen. Now that he has
the diploma, he uses the magic the way a chef uses a good knife: as a tool in service of the
real work.

## Role in the town

Fenwick runs the tavern and the kitchen. He is the outpost's chef, its social anchor, and the
person most likely to know how everyone is doing on any given day. People talk to Fenwick because
he is easy to talk to, and because there is usually something cooking.

Mechanically, he is the source of day-long meal buffs for the party. As the Tavern building
upgrades and his recipe collection grows, the range and quality of those buffs expand. He is
also the party's second spellcaster, bringing arcane firepower that complements Tharr's divine
support.

## Background

Fenwick's parents were respectable halflings who wanted their son to make something of himself,
which in their view meant wizard college. Fenwick went, and he did well enough, but he spent
more time in the dormitory kitchen than the library. He graduated with a solid grasp of
evocation and transmutation and an even more solid grasp of how to properly reduce a sauce.

He attended the academy alongside the player, where the two became friends despite their
different temperaments. Fenwick studied magic while the player trained as a fighter in the
next hall over. When the frontier posting came through, Fenwick volunteered. He heard there
was a kitchen to be built from scratch, which sounded like the opportunity of a lifetime. A
chance to design a menu from the ground up, forage new ingredients, and cook for people who
genuinely need a good meal. The fact that the borderlands are dangerous only makes the cooking
more important, in his view. Morale starts at the table.

## Arrival

Starting party. Travels with the player to the outpost.
